# Summer research inquiry

Subject: Summer research inquiry - efficient local LLM inference

Dear Professor,

I am a student interested in efficient machine learning systems, and I am writing
to ask whether you might consider me for a summer research opportunity in your
group.

I recently completed a local experiment examining how prompt instructions affect
tokens, test success and estimated CPU package energy in Qwen2.5-Coder 0.5B and
1.5B. The study contains 560 primary requests across 40 HumanEval tasks and seven
prompt variants, plus 280 requests for repeat diagnostics. I have attached a
report describing the setup, paired comparisons, failure analysis and limitations.

The clearest observation was that adding "Return only code" substantially reduced
output length and estimated request energy relative to the minimal instruction.
Accuracy differences remained uncertain, and baseline drift limits the energy
claims. Investigating these limitations taught me why careful measurement and
verified task success matter when assessing inference efficiency.

I would like to develop this work through a more controlled study of output
constraints and examples, prefix-cache reuse, or CPU inference optimization at a
fixed quality target. I would value your advice on which direction could make a
useful research contribution, and whether there may be a suitable project in
your group.

If you are open to it, I would appreciate a brief discussion. I would be happy
to share my CV, code and additional experimental details.

Thank you for your time and consideration.

Best regards,
Dhruv Brahmbhatt
